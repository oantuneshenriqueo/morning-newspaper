# Extras

Two boxes at the end of the edition, written in the reader's language. Both are short (up to 2 sentences) and must be true: confirm before using, never write from memory alone.

## On this day
- Something that happened on this date (day and month) in a past year.
- Prefer, in order: technology and computing, the reader's country and region, science, culture.
- Format: "In [year], [what happened]." plus, if it fits, one sentence on why it matters.
- The collection already brings Wikipedia candidates (`h1`, `h2`…), filtered and ranked by `history_keywords`; Wikipedia counts as confirmation. Rewrite the fact in your own words.
- Only if there are no candidates: search "[day month] in history" (in the reader's language) and confirm the year in a second source if unclear.
- Avoid wars, tragedies and deaths as the main fact.

## Did you know?
- A curiosity from computing or technology (or the reader's main interest): history of programming, how something works inside, origin of a term, a curious record.
- Confirm it with one web search before using, and put the confirming URL in `did_you_know.url`.
- Do not repeat: the collection lists the ones already used.
- Light, almanac-like tone, no exaggeration.

## When it does not work out
If a search does not confirm a fact, pick another. If nothing is confirmed, the box is left out.
